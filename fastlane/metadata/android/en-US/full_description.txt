JotLock is a fast, no-nonsense notes app built around one idea: all the magic starts from the lock screen. A persistent Quick note notification puts a Write note button there, an inline Jot field waits in the notification shade, and per-note reminders always play your sound but never clutter your lock screen.

Features:
* Quick note from the lock screen - capture a thought in seconds
* Inline Jot reply straight from the notification shade
* Reminders with exact alarms: once, daily, weekly, or custom repeat
* Checklists with [ ] / [x] syntax and progress counts
* Priority colors, pinning, copy and share
* Optional App PIN to keep notes private
* Search as you type
* JSON and text export, JSON import - your data stays yours
* Choose what happens after saving: lock the phone, go home, or stay on the confirmation

Free, open source (MIT), and ad-free. No accounts, no cloud, no INTERNET permission: notes physically have nowhere to go but the device.
